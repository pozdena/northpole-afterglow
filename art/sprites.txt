# North Pole sprites. One block per sprite: "@name", then rows of pixels, then a blank line.
# Every sprite faces RIGHT; the module mirrors it for walking left.
#
#  .  transparent          K  black          W  white          w  snow shadow (light gray)
#  D  dark gray            R  red            r  dark red       G  green
#  g  dark green           B  brown          b  dark brown     Y  yellow
#  O  orange               S  skin           L  light blue     U  blue
#  P  pink                 T  tan            M  magenta        C  cyan
#  N  gold
#  1 2 3 4  per-instance color slots (an elf's suit and hat, a reindeer's nose ...)
#  *  twinkling light (cycles through the light colors)
#  !  lit window (warm yellow, flickers now and then)
#  s  settled snow on a tree: white, but only once it has snowed in this scene; before
#     that nothing, or the sprite's bare color ("@tree_small bare=G": branch tips)
